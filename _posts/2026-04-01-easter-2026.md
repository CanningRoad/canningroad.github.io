We have our **Good Friday** service on Friday 3rd April at **10:30am** - this will be for all ages and experiences, with something for everyone.

We'll look at the cross, the turning point of history, when the Servant King laid down his life for his friends (that's you and me) 😊

Our **Easter Sunday** service will also be at **10:30am** when we celebrate that Jesus didn't stay in the tomb... he conquered death and rose again!

These will be short-ish services to allow you to go on with your Easter family life. We hope you can join us.

Happy Easter! 🕊
